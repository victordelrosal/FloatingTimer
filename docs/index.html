Always On Top

Stays visible over ALL apps, including Keynote and PowerPoint presentation mode.

Drag Anywhere

Position it flush against any corner or anywhere on your screen.

Click to Edit

Tap minutes or seconds to quickly set any custom time.

Visual Warnings

Background smoothly transitions to red as time runs low.

Alarm Sound

Repeating alert when timer ends. Click anywhere to dismiss.
